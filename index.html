Welcome to ReBuild Materials

ReBuild Materials gives homeowners, contractors, and builders access to quality reclaimed and reusable building materials at affordable prices.


We help reduce construction waste by giving useful building materials a second life.

Why ReBuild Materials?

Construction materials do not always need to end up in a landfill. ReBuild Materials connects usable materials with people who can give them another purpose.


Our goal is to provide affordable materials while supporting a more sustainable approach to construction.
